**Why the specification is Apache 2.0.** Apache 2.0 includes a patent grant, so anyone implementing the specification receives a license to the contributors' patents that the specification necessarily uses. CC BY 4.0 grants no patent rights. This matches the Model Context Protocol, which licenses its code and specification under Apache 2.0 and its other documentation under CC BY 4.0.

**The registry data** stays under CC BY 4.0 for now: you may share and adapt it for any purpose, including commercially, as long as you give appropriate credit, link to the license and indicate changes. A suggested attribution:

> Reversibility Classes registry, by Deepak Waghmare and contributors, https://github.com/TwoMuchLabs/reversibility-classes, licensed under CC BY 4.0.

**History.** Before 25 October 2026 the specification and schema were licensed under CC BY 4.0. Versions published before that date remain available under CC BY 4.0; from v0.3.1 they are licensed under Apache 2.0. Every contribution up to that change was made by the maintainer.

Contributions are accepted under the license of the files they change, certified with a [Developer Certificate of Origin](CONTRIBUTING.md#sign-off-developer-certificate-of-origin) sign-off.

If you use the specification in research, please cite it using [`CITATION.cff`](CITATION.cff).
